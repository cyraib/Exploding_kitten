## Rules and data cautions

- Every deck has `README.md`, `instructions.md`, `deck.json`, `cards.csv`, `asset-map.md`, `rules.pdf`, `photo.webp`, and `logo.webp`. Recipes for Disaster also has `field-guide.pdf` and `recipes/`.
- `deck.json` is the structured inventory; `cards.csv` mirrors it. Asset paths inside these files are relative to the project root. Markdown links are relative to their containing file. Rulebooks are the edition-specific authorities; instruction files are paraphrases.
- Shared artwork variants do not establish physical copy counts or exact edition print assignments. Preserve original bytes and filenames, including source spelling errors.
- Good vs. Evil has 55 physical cards: 53 ordinary-deck cards plus Godcat and Devilcat, which start on the playmat.
- Party Pack selects 44 paw-marked or 67 unmarked non-Kitten cards, or both, according to player count. Zombie Kittens selects 24 marked or 33 unmarked non-Kitten cards, or both. Derive subsets from `with_paw` / `without_paw`, excluding Exploding Kittens.
- Barking documentation uses the explosion effect; older artwork may show hand exchange. Defuse setup, death presentation, hazard counts, and NOW timing vary by edition. Do not normalize them into one universal rule.
- Three existing recipe issues remain: Attack of the Attacks exceeds box supply for Super Skip at 3 players and Targeted Attack at 5 players; Sticky Fingers has a 5-player starting-hand discrepancy. Printed recipe booklets need review. The combined 2-9-player Zombie Apocalypse setup is not fully specified locally.
