# Exploding Kittens: Zombie Kittens - instructions

Paraphrased gameplay reference. [Original PDF](rules.pdf) is the authoritative source for this edition; preserve its diagrams and printed exceptions when resolving a dispute. [Inventory and asset links](README.md).

## Contents and requirements

61 cards; 2-5 standalone; 2-9 in the linked combined-game variant players. No separate accessory is listed.

## Setup

1. Set aside all four Exploding Kittens.
2. For two players use the 24 paw-marked non-Kitten cards; for three use the 33 unmarked non-Kitten cards; for four/five use all 57 non-Kitten cards.
3. Remove Zombie Kittens from the selected set and give each player one. In a four-player game shuffle the one extra Zombie Kitten back into the deck; other supported player counts consume all selected Zombie Kittens.
4. Shuffle and deal seven other cards per player, making eight including their Zombie Kitten.
5. Add players-minus-one Exploding Kittens; remove unused Kittens. Shuffle and form a face-down draw pile, leaving discard space.
6. Choose the first player and begin clockwise, skipping dead players' normal turns.

## Turn, survival, and winning

Keep your hand private. On your turn, play any number of playable cards face up onto the discard pile, resolving each one, or pass. Unless a card ends the turn another way, draw the top card to finish the turn. Play starts clockwise. Drawing happens at the end, not the beginning.

Immediately reveal an Exploding Kitten. Use the edition's rescue card as described below or resolve that edition's death procedure. When you Defuse a drawn Exploding Kitten, discard the Defuse and secretly reinsert the Kitten anywhere in the draw pile without looking at or rearranging other cards. That ends one turn; any remaining attacked turns still have to be taken.

The last surviving player wins. Hand size has no upper or lower limit; an empty hand does not eliminate a player. You may count the draw pile. Saving cards early is a suggested strategy, not a rule.

## Combos and timing

A matching pair of cat cards steals one random card. With the PDF's special-combo rules, any two cards sharing a title can do this; three sharing a title let you request a named card from a chosen player, who gives you one if they have it. Ignore the cards' individual effects when spending them as a combo. These PDFs do not establish a universal five-different-card combo.

Nope may cancel a card action or combo before it starts, even outside your turn; another Nope cancels the preceding Nope. Spent and canceled cards stay discarded. Nope does not stop an Exploding Kitten or a rescue card. Cards marked NOW may be used between actions, but not midway through an action. Edition-specific restrictions below take precedence.

Attack transfers the current and any remaining untaken turns and adds the new attack amount. For example, responding immediately to a two-turn Attack with another two-turn Attack gives the next victim four turns; completing one turn first gives them three. Skip ends one required turn; Super Skip ends all of them.

## Edition-specific procedures and variants

- Zombie Kitten replaces Defuse in this standalone deck; there are zero ordinary Defuses. Use Zombie Kitten's procedure instead of the common Defuse paragraph above.
- On drawing an Exploding Kitten you may use a Zombie Kitten, but you can deliberately die even if you have one. Place the lethal Kitten face up in front of you and retain your hidden hand.
- Dead players skip normal turns. They can still use Nope and cards explicitly marked NOW between actions. They cannot play ordinary action cards merely because those cards affect dead players.
- Living players cannot steal from dead players through Favor or matching combos; only effects explicitly addressing dead players may reach them.
- Whenever you use a Zombie Kitten and somebody is dead, you must revive exactly one of your choice. Secretly reinsert your own lethal Kitten and the revived player's Kitten separately, potentially at different positions. If nobody is dead, reinsert only yours.
- The last living player wins immediately, even if dead players still hold rescue cards.
- Paw cats: Cat-O-Lantern and De-Cat-Ipated have four marked copies each. Unmarked cats: Electrocat and Vampire Cat have four copies each. The PDF gives aggregate 8+8 cat counts; the individual split is sourced from the website's structured inventory.
- Attack of the Dead requires at least one dead player and assigns three turns per dead player. Grave Robber also requires dead players but is not NOW. Feed the Dead is NOW and requires a dead target. Clone cannot be played while dead or outside your turn, even when copying a NOW card.
- The combined Zombie Apocalypse option allows 2-9 players by combining this with another Exploding Kittens game. The supplied PDF refers to the official tutorial for exact combined setup/interactions; do not extrapolate the standalone paw/dealing rules without those instructions.

## Card reference

The counts in the inventory include cards set aside during setup. Cat names sharing the cat-card family all use the same pair/combo rules.

### Attack 2x

End your turn without drawing and give the next player two turns. If played while under an Attack, transfer all current/remaining untaken turns and add two. Each required turn is played or passed, then ended by drawing or a valid turn-ending card.

### Attack of the Dead

With at least one dead player, end your turn without drawing and give the next living player three turns per dead player. Attack chaining transfers remaining turns and adds the new card's attack amount. Cannot be played when nobody is dead.

### Cat-O-Lantern, De-Cat-Ipated, Electrocat, Vampire Cat

A single cat has no effect. Discard two with the same cat name to steal one random card. The optional pair/triple combos extend matching to any shared title; a triple requests a named card and fails if the target lacks it. Feral Cat can replace a powerless cat. Cat identities are not interchangeable without a wildcard.

### Clairvoyance

Play when another player rescues themselves from an Exploding Kitten to watch where the Kitten(s) are reinserted. NOW timing permits use by living or dead players outside their turn. You may move over to watch the insertion.

### Clone

On your turn, place it over the top discard; it takes that card's effect. Cannot copy another Clone. Cannot be used outside your turn or while dead, even to copy a NOW card. A cat-plus-Clone pair works only when you play both the cat and Clone yourself.

### Dig Deeper

Inspect the top draw-pile card. Either keep it, or draw and keep the next card no matter what it is, then return the rejected first card to the top. End one turn.

### Exploding Kitten

Reveal it. You may play a Zombie Kitten or choose to die. If dead, place this Kitten face up in front of you and retain your hidden hand for possible revival.

### Favor

Choose another player; they choose one card from their hand to give you. In Zombie Kittens, the target must be living. A held Exploding Kitten can be given away under Streaking rules, which makes the recipient resolve its explosion.

### Feed The Dead

Choose a dead player. Every living player with cards except you gives that player one chosen card. Requires a dead player. NOW timing permits living/dead players to play it outside their turn, before an action starts.

### Grave Robber

Every dead player with cards contributes one chosen card from their hand to the draw pile; shuffle it. Cannot be played if nobody is dead. This is not a NOW card.

### Nope

Cancel an action or combo before it begins; another Nope reverses it. Living or dead players can play it outside their turn. Cannot stop an Exploding Kitten or Zombie Kitten.

### See the Future 3x

Privately inspect the top three cards of the draw pile and return them in the same order. Do not reveal them to others. Continue your turn; this does not replace your final draw.

### Shuffle NOW

Shuffle the draw pile. NOW timing permits use before an action starts even outside your turn, including by dead players in Zombie Kittens. Does not change whose turn it is.

### Skip

End one turn without drawing. An Attack with two required turns needs two Skips to avoid both draws.

### Super Skip

End all your required turns without drawing, including outstanding turns from an Attack.

### Zombie Kitten

After drawing an Exploding Kitten, you may discard Zombie Kitten to survive, but using it is optional. If any player is dead, revive exactly one of your choice. Secretly reinsert your lethal Kitten and that player's lethal Kitten in independently chosen positions without inspecting/reordering other cards. If nobody is dead, only reinsert your own Kitten. Ends one turn. Cannot be Noped.
