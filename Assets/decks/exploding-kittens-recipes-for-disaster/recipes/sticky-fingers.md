# Sticky Fingers

[Recipe catalog](README.md). [Official source](https://www.explodingkittens.com/pages/recipes?name=sticky-fingers). Approximate play time: 15 minutes. These ingredient counts are an online supplement, not the whole-box inventory.

## Source conflicts

Do not silently substitute missing cards or assume these are ready-to-play validated setups.

- The 2021 PDF example shows a five-player starting-hand exception of five regular cards; online data uses seven for all player counts. Check the printed recipe booklet.
