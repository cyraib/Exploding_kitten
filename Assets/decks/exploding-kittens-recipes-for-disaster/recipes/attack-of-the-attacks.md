# Attack Of The Attacks

[Recipe catalog](README.md). [Official source](https://www.explodingkittens.com/pages/recipes?name=attack-of-the-attacks). Approximate play time: 15 minutes. These ingredient counts are an online supplement, not the whole-box inventory.

## Source conflicts

Do not silently substitute missing cards or assume these are ready-to-play validated setups.

- 3 players: Super Skip requests 4; box contains 2.
- 5 players: Targeted Attack 2x requests 6; box contains 4.
