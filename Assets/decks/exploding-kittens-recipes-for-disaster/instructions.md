# Exploding Kittens: Recipes for Disaster - instructions

Paraphrased gameplay reference. [Original PDF](rules.pdf) is the authoritative source for this edition; preserve its diagrams and printed exceptions when resolving a dispute. [Inventory and asset links](README.md).

## Contents and requirements

121 cards; 2-5; selected recipe may narrow the range players. 13 printed recipe booklets/cards; 5 blank recipe booklets/cards; Cone of Shame; separate Field Guide

## Setup

1. Choose a recipe matching your player count. The box's pre-bundled Nope Sauce recipe is suggested for first use; the entire 121-card inventory is not a single default play deck.
2. Build the deck from the recipe's exact ingredients. A 2x/3x/5x in a title describes the card effect, not its copy quantity. Player-dependent expressions such as players-minus-two must be evaluated separately.
3. Remove the recipe's Exploding Kittens, Imploding Kittens, and Defuses before dealing.
4. Give each player the recipe's stated number of Defuses and put any remaining included Defuses into the deck. Shuffle and deal the recipe's stated number of ordinary starting cards.
5. Insert the exact Exploding and Imploding Kittens listed by the recipe. Do not universally replace these counts with players-minus-one; recipes with Streaking, Imploding, or deliberate extra hazards differ.
6. Shuffle, place the draw pile face down with discard space, choose a first player, and follow the recipe's own exceptions.

## Turn, survival, and winning

Keep your hand private. On your turn, play any number of playable cards face up onto the discard pile, resolving each one, or pass. Unless a card ends the turn another way, draw the top card to finish the turn. Play starts clockwise. Drawing happens at the end, not the beginning.

Immediately reveal an Exploding Kitten. Use the edition's rescue card as described below or resolve that edition's death procedure. When you Defuse a drawn Exploding Kitten, discard the Defuse and secretly reinsert the Kitten anywhere in the draw pile without looking at or rearranging other cards. That ends one turn; any remaining attacked turns still have to be taken.

The last surviving player wins. Hand size has no upper or lower limit; an empty hand does not eliminate a player. You may count the draw pile. Saving cards early is a suggested strategy, not a rule.

## Combos and timing

A matching pair of cat cards steals one random card. With the PDF's special-combo rules, any two cards sharing a title can do this; three sharing a title let you request a named card from a chosen player, who gives you one if they have it. Ignore the cards' individual effects when spending them as a combo. These PDFs do not establish a universal five-different-card combo.

Nope may cancel a card action or combo before it starts, even outside your turn; another Nope cancels the preceding Nope. Spent and canceled cards stay discarded. Nope does not stop an Exploding Kitten or a rescue card. Cards marked NOW may be used between actions, but not midway through an action. Edition-specific restrictions below take precedence.

Attack transfers the current and any remaining untaken turns and adds the new attack amount. For example, responding immediately to a two-turn Attack with another two-turn Attack gives the next victim four turns; completing one turn first gives them three. Skip ends one required turn; Super Skip ends all of them.

## Edition-specific procedures and variants

- The box is an ingredient library from the base game and three expansions. Each recipe makes its own standalone deck, which may intentionally omit otherwise familiar cards.
- Printed recipe booklets control both ingredients and initial hand size; five blank booklets support custom recipes. Do not count them among the 121 game cards.
- A separate official Field Guide PDF was downloaded to this folder because the supplied main PDF does not contain a full per-card effect/count reference. Its April 2025 file is labeled as a supplement, not a replacement for the 2021 main sheet.
- The first person to forget the next player or direction wears the Cone of Shame as a direction indicator for the rest of the game. Turn it whenever Reverse changes direction.
- The main PDF shows Sticky Fingers as a sample recipe: Exploding Kittens = players; Defuses = players+1; Attack 2x, Skip, I'll Take That, Mark, Tacocat, Rainbow-Ralphing Cat, Zombie Cat, and Beard Cat = four each; Streaking Kitten = one; Feral Cat = six. A five-player starting-hand exception is visible in the sample illustration. See the source-conflict note in recipes/sticky-fingers.md.
- The online recipe catalog adds 15 published setups with per-player ingredient counts. It is supplemental: it does not establish which exact 13 booklets were packaged with this box printing. Some published ingredient lists exceed box counts; affected recipes carry explicit conflict notes.
- Use the Field Guide's explosion version of Barking Kitten. Its separate death presentation places lethal cards in front of dead players, unlike the older discard-everything base sheet. It does not grant Zombie-style revival.

## Card reference

The counts in the inventory include cards set aside during setup. Cat names sharing the cat-card family all use the same pair/combo rules.

### Alter the Future 3x

Privately inspect and reorder the top three draw-pile cards, return them face down, and continue your turn. Do not show them to others.

### Alter the Future 3x NOW

Privately inspect/reorder the top three cards and return them face down. May be played between actions even outside your turn. Does not change whose turn it is; cannot interrupt an action already in progress.

### Attack 2x

End your turn without drawing and give the next player two turns. If played while under an Attack, transfer all current/remaining untaken turns and add two. Each required turn is played or passed, then ended by drawing or a valid turn-ending card.

### Barking Kitten

Put it face up before you and ask who has its mate. If someone has it, that player must Defuse or explode; discard both Barking Kittens at the end of the turn. If nobody has the mate, your card stays before you outside your hand and makes you the target when the mate is played. If you obtain both, choose any target. Playing it does not end your turn. Cannot be Noped. Defusing this effect does not put a card into the draw pile. This is the explosion version in the supplied PDFs, not the older hand-exchange printing.

### Bury

Draw a card to end one turn, inspect it, then secretly reinsert it anywhere in the draw pile rather than retaining it. Cannot be used while I'll Take That is in front of you. When burying an Imploding Kitten, preserve the face-up/face-down orientation in which it was drawn.

### Beard Cat, Rainbow-Ralphing Cat, Tacocat, Zombie Cat

A single cat has no effect. Discard two with the same cat name to steal one random card. The optional pair/triple combos extend matching to any shared title; a triple requests a named card and fails if the target lacks it. Feral Cat can replace a powerless cat. Cat identities are not interchangeable without a wildcard.

### Catomic Bomb

Remove and reveal all Exploding Kittens from the draw pile, shuffle the remaining draw pile, and place those Exploding Kittens face down on top. End one turn without drawing. Do not remove Imploding Kittens.

### Defuse

After drawing an Exploding Kitten, discard this to survive and secretly reinsert that Kitten anywhere in the draw pile without inspecting or rearranging other cards. Ends one turn, not every outstanding attacked turn. Cannot be Noped. Special Barking/Devilcat explosions consume a Defuse without reinserting a card.

### Draw From the Bottom

Finish one turn by drawing the bottom card instead of the top. Resolve that card normally. Under an Attack, this satisfies only one of the required turns.

### Exploding Kitten

Reveal immediately. Defuse it or die. The referenced field guide keeps the lethal Kitten face up in front of the dead player and the rest of their hand face down in front of them.

### Feral Cat

A wildcard for powerless cat cards in matching cat combos. It cannot stand in for a non-cat action card such as Attack or Shuffle.

### Garbage Collection

Every player with cards, including you, chooses one card to put secretly into the draw pile; shuffle the pile afterward. A protected held Exploding Kitten may be contributed. Players with empty hands do not contribute.

### I'll Take That

Put it face up before another player who does not already have one. On their next draw, they inspect the drawn card privately and give it to you; then discard I'll Take That. If it is an Exploding Kitten, you must resolve it. The targeted player has still completed their draw and turn.

### Imploding Kitten

If drawn face down, secretly reinsert it face up anywhere in the draw pile without spending a Defuse. Drawing it face up kills you immediately: neither Defuse nor Nope can save you. In the expansion PDF, discard it after it kills a player. It has a visible white border when face up. A Streaking Kitten cannot protect you from it. Shuffle face-up Imploding cards out of sight. The supplementary field guide keeps a lethal Imploding Kitten face up before the dead player and their other cards face down.

### Mark

Randomly choose one card in a target player's hand and return it facing outward so others can see it. The owner may still look at it. Keep it marked until it leaves that hand; a stolen card does not stay marked.

### Nope

Cancel an action or combo before it begins, even outside your turn. Another Nope cancels a Nope, with successive Nopes alternating cancellation. The action's cards and all Nopes remain discarded. Exploding Kittens and Defuses cannot be Noped. Do not interrupt an action after it has begun.

### Personal Attack 3x

Attack yourself for three turns: continue the current turn normally and take two more after it ends. If another Attack is played while turns are still outstanding, transfer the remaining turns and add the new Attack's amount.

### Reverse

Reverse play direction and end one turn without drawing. With two players it acts as Skip. During an Attack it reverses direction but only satisfies one required turn. Update the Cone of Shame's direction arrow if it is being used.

### See the Future 3x

Privately inspect the top three cards of the draw pile and return them in the same order. Do not reveal them to others. Continue your turn; this does not replace your final draw.

### See the Future 5x

Privately inspect the top five cards and return them in the same order without showing others. Continue your turn.

### Share the Future 3x

Privately inspect/reorder the top three cards, show them to the next player only, then return them face down and continue your turn.

### Shuffle

Randomize the draw pile without learning its final order. The Original/Party Pack/2-Player/Cat Burglar PDFs say to shuffle thoroughly; NSFW and Good vs. Evil say until the next player tells you to stop. If an Imploding Kitten is face up, shuffle out of sight so its resulting position is unknown.

### Skip

End one turn without drawing. An Attack with two required turns needs two Skips to avoid both draws.

### Streaking Kitten

While kept in your hand, protects one secretly held Exploding Kitten. If Streaking leaves while an unprotected Exploding Kitten remains, Defuse it or die. A player receiving a held Exploding Kitten must Defuse or die; this does not replace their normal turn draw. Playing a held Exploding Kitten into the discard also causes an explosion. Does not protect against Imploding Kittens. In editions with a five-card combo, neither Streaking nor Exploding Kittens can be spent or recovered through that combo.

### Super Skip

End all your required turns without drawing, including outstanding turns from an Attack.

### Swap Top and Bottom

Swap the top and bottom cards of the draw pile without looking at them. This does not end your turn.

### Targeted Attack 2x

End your current turn(s) without drawing and choose another player to take two turns; play continues from that victim. If responding to an Attack, transfer all untaken turns and add two. Does not require targeting your immediate neighbor.
