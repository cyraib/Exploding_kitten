# Barking Kittens: Expansion - instructions

Paraphrased gameplay reference. [Original PDF](rules.pdf) is the authoritative source for this edition; preserve its diagrams and printed exceptions when resolving a dispute. [Inventory and asset links](README.md).

## Contents and requirements

20 cards; base-game player range; illustrated Original-deck setup supports 2-5 players. Tower of Power crown; requires an Exploding Kittens base deck

## Setup

1. For the illustrated Original-deck setup, set aside the four base Exploding Kittens, six base Defuses, and this expansion's one Tower of Power card.
2. Give each player one Defuse and shuffle all extra Defuses back into the main deck, as this sheet specifies.
3. Shuffle the other 19 expansion cards into the main deck.
4. Deal six cards to the table and cover them with the Tower of Power crown to create the stash. Do not treat these as a player's hand.
5. Return the Tower of Power card to the deck and shuffle. Deal seven other cards per player, making eight with their Defuse.
6. Add players-minus-one Exploding Kittens and remove unused ones. Shuffle, form the face-down draw pile with discard space, and choose a first player.
7. When combining expansions, apply each expansion's setup adjustments; Streaking or Imploding changes the hazard requirement.

## Turn, survival, and winning

Keep your hand private. On your turn, play any number of playable cards face up onto the discard pile, resolving each one, or pass. Unless a card ends the turn another way, draw the top card to finish the turn. Play starts clockwise. Drawing happens at the end, not the beginning.

Immediately reveal an Exploding Kitten. Use the edition's rescue card as described below or resolve that edition's death procedure. When you Defuse a drawn Exploding Kitten, discard the Defuse and secretly reinsert the Kitten anywhere in the draw pile without looking at or rearranging other cards. That ends one turn; any remaining attacked turns still have to be taken.

The last surviving player wins. Hand size has no upper or lower limit; an empty hand does not eliminate a player. You may count the draw pile. Saving cards early is a suggested strategy, not a rule.

## Combos and timing

A matching pair of cat cards steals one random card. With the PDF's special-combo rules, any two cards sharing a title can do this; three sharing a title let you request a named card from a chosen player, who gives you one if they have it. Ignore the cards' individual effects when spending them as a combo. These PDFs do not establish a universal five-different-card combo.

Nope may cancel a card action or combo before it starts, even outside your turn; another Nope cancels the preceding Nope. Spent and canceled cards stay discarded. Nope does not stop an Exploding Kitten or a rescue card. Cards marked NOW may be used between actions, but not midway through an action. Edition-specific restrictions below take precedence.

Attack transfers the current and any remaining untaken turns and adds the new attack amount. For example, responding immediately to a two-turn Attack with another two-turn Attack gives the next victim four turns; completing one turn first gives them three. Skip ends one required turn; Super Skip ends all of them.

## Edition-specific procedures and variants

- This is an expansion. [Original Edition instructions](../exploding-kittens-original-edition/instructions.md) cover normal turns and common cards. This supplied sheet returns all leftover Defuses; do not silently substitute the base sheet's two/three-player exception.
- The supplied Barking Kitten effect is Defuse-or-explode. Some older card artwork contains a half-hand exchange rule. Preserve that image as an original asset, but use this PDF's effect for the documented version.
- Barking Kitten is not a turn-ending card. A lone played Barking stays visible outside its owner's hand until its mate triggers an explosion; both are then discarded at the end of the turn. A Defuse prevents that explosion without reinserting any card.
- The Tower of Power's stash redirects theft until empty. Its wearer cannot inspect/absorb the stash into their own hand. The six stash cards are excluded from the dealt hands and initial draw pile.
- Bury cannot be played when I'll Take That is in front of you. When burying Imploding, preserve its drawn orientation rather than applying the ordinary first-draw flip.
- NOW Alter the Future can be played before another action/draw starts, but cannot interrupt a shuffle already happening. Personal Attack keeps the current turn and adds two more.
- With Curse of the Cat Butt: blind Barking is resolved normally; a blind target blindly plays until Defuse, losing non-Defuses. After successful rescue, return to non-blind play. Keep any Barking displayed in front of a blind player visible. After resolution both players may inspect their hands for unprotected Exploding Kittens that must be defused.
- Blind Tower: stash cards are not affected until they enter a blind player's hand. Blind I'll Take That target: inspect the drawn card before giving it away, then become unblind after the successful draw. If blind and receiving through I'll Take That, use blind Favor's received-card procedure.
- Blind Potluck: choose a contribution without looking, then you may inspect the chosen card to resolve loss of Streaking protection.
- The faster variant removes approximately one third of the non-Kitten deck before inserting Exploding Kittens and the Tower of Power card; follow the rest of setup, including the stash. The sheet's wording requires keeping those protected setup cards out of the random removal.

## Card reference

The counts in the inventory include cards set aside during setup. Cat names sharing the cat-card family all use the same pair/combo rules.

### Alter the Future 3x NOW

Privately inspect/reorder the top three cards and return them face down. May be played between actions even outside your turn. Does not change whose turn it is; cannot interrupt an action already in progress.

### Barking Kitten

Put it face up before you and ask who has its mate. If someone has it, that player must Defuse or explode; discard both Barking Kittens at the end of the turn. If nobody has the mate, your card stays before you outside your hand and makes you the target when the mate is played. If you obtain both, choose any target. Playing it does not end your turn. Cannot be Noped. Defusing this effect does not put a card into the draw pile. This is the explosion version in the supplied PDFs, not the older hand-exchange printing.

### Bury

Draw a card to end one turn, inspect it, then secretly reinsert it anywhere in the draw pile rather than retaining it. Cannot be used while I'll Take That is in front of you. When burying an Imploding Kitten, preserve the face-up/face-down orientation in which it was drawn.

### I'll Take That

Put it face up before another player who does not already have one. On their next draw, they inspect the drawn card privately and give it to you; then discard I'll Take That. If it is an Exploding Kitten, you must resolve it. The targeted player has still completed their draw and turn.

### Personal Attack 3x

Attack yourself for three turns: continue the current turn normally and take two more after it ends. If another Attack is played while turns are still outstanding, transfer the remaining turns and add the new Attack's amount.

### Potluck

Starting with you and following current play direction, each player places one chosen card from their hand face down on top of the draw pile. This does not itself end your turn.

### Share the Future 3x

Privately inspect/reorder the top three cards, show them to the next player only, then return them face down and continue your turn.

### Super Skip

End all your required turns without drawing, including outstanding turns from an Attack.

### Tower of Power

Put on the crown and move the six stash cards into it without looking. Theft from you takes random cards from the stash instead of your hand until the stash is empty, including Favor, pairs, triples, and other stealing. Never transfer stash cards into your own hand. Keep wearing the crown for the rest of the game.
