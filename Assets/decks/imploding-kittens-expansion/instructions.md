# Imploding Kittens: Expansion - instructions

Paraphrased gameplay reference. [Original PDF](rules.pdf) is the authoritative source for this edition; preserve its diagrams and printed exceptions when resolving a dispute. [Inventory and asset links](README.md).

## Contents and requirements

20 cards; base-game range; up to 6 with an Original 56-card deck players. Cone of Shame; requires an Exploding Kittens base deck

## Setup

1. For the illustrated Original-deck setup, remove the expansion's one Imploding Kitten and the base deck's four Exploding Kittens and six Defuses.
2. Shuffle the other 19 expansion cards into the remaining main deck. The sheet allows selectively including expansion cards instead of using all 19.
3. Deal seven regular cards per player, then give one Defuse each, making eight-card hands.
4. Add the Imploding Kitten plus enough Exploding Kittens that their combined total is players-minus-one; remove unused Exploding Kittens. The printed two-player variant instead uses one Exploding plus one Imploding Kitten, and the first death loses.
5. Return unused Defuses; for two or three players return only two extras and remove the remainder. Shuffle the draw pile face down, leave discard space, and choose a first player.
6. If also using Streaking, make the combined Exploding-plus-Imploding total equal the number of players, following Streaking's extra-hazard rule.

## Turn, survival, and winning

Keep your hand private. On your turn, play any number of playable cards face up onto the discard pile, resolving each one, or pass. Unless a card ends the turn another way, draw the top card to finish the turn. Play starts clockwise. Drawing happens at the end, not the beginning.

Immediately reveal an Exploding Kitten. Use the edition's rescue card as described below or resolve that edition's death procedure. When you Defuse a drawn Exploding Kitten, discard the Defuse and secretly reinsert the Kitten anywhere in the draw pile without looking at or rearranging other cards. That ends one turn; any remaining attacked turns still have to be taken.

The last surviving player wins. Hand size has no upper or lower limit; an empty hand does not eliminate a player. You may count the draw pile. Saving cards early is a suggested strategy, not a rule.

## Combos and timing

A matching pair of cat cards steals one random card. With the PDF's special-combo rules, any two cards sharing a title can do this; three sharing a title let you request a named card from a chosen player, who gives you one if they have it. Ignore the cards' individual effects when spending them as a combo. These PDFs do not establish a universal five-different-card combo.

Nope may cancel a card action or combo before it starts, even outside your turn; another Nope cancels the preceding Nope. Spent and canceled cards stay discarded. Nope does not stop an Exploding Kitten or a rescue card. Cards marked NOW may be used between actions, but not midway through an action. Edition-specific restrictions below take precedence.

Attack transfers the current and any remaining untaken turns and adds the new attack amount. For example, responding immediately to a two-turn Attack with another two-turn Attack gives the next victim four turns; completing one turn first gives them three. Skip ends one required turn; Super Skip ends all of them.

## Edition-specific procedures and variants

- This is an expansion, not a standalone 20-card game. [Original Edition instructions](../exploding-kittens-original-edition/instructions.md) supply normal turns, Defuse, Nope, and matching rules.
- The Imploding Kitten can raise Original Edition's capacity to six, or replace an Exploding Kitten. On first face-down draw, secretly reinsert it face up without Defuse. Its next face-up draw eliminates the player regardless of Defuse; it cannot be Noped. Discard it after that death under this expansion's sheet.
- If a face-up Imploding Kitten is on top when shuffling, shuffle out of sight to avoid knowing its new position. Its white border distinguishes it when face up.
- Reverse ends one turn and reverses direction; with two players it acts as Skip. If attacked, it only finishes one outstanding turn.
- The first player who forgets the next player becomes the direction indicator and wears the Cone of Shame for the rest of the game. Rotate its arrow whenever Reverse changes direction.
- The optional faster variant removes roughly one third of the remaining non-Kitten draw deck after dealing and before inserting the required Kittens. Insert those Kittens and shuffle afterward.
- Streaking does not protect against Imploding. Using several expansions requires honoring all their setup adjustments, rather than blindly appending every card.

## Card reference

The counts in the inventory include cards set aside during setup. Cat names sharing the cat-card family all use the same pair/combo rules.

### Alter the Future 3x

Privately inspect and reorder the top three draw-pile cards, return them face down, and continue your turn. Do not show them to others.

### Draw From the Bottom

Finish one turn by drawing the bottom card instead of the top. Resolve that card normally. Under an Attack, this satisfies only one of the required turns.

### Feral Cat

A wildcard for powerless cat cards in matching cat combos. It cannot stand in for a non-cat action card such as Attack or Shuffle.

### Imploding Kitten

If drawn face down, secretly reinsert it face up anywhere in the draw pile without spending a Defuse. Drawing it face up kills you immediately: neither Defuse nor Nope can save you. In the expansion PDF, discard it after it kills a player. It has a visible white border when face up. A Streaking Kitten cannot protect you from it. Shuffle face-up Imploding cards out of sight.

### Reverse

Reverse play direction and end one turn without drawing. With two players it acts as Skip. During an Attack it reverses direction but only satisfies one required turn. Update the Cone of Shame's direction arrow if it is being used.

### Targeted Attack 2x

End your current turn(s) without drawing and choose another player to take two turns; play continues from that victim. If responding to an Attack, transfer all untaken turns and add two. Does not require targeting your immediate neighbor.
