# Streaking Kittens: Expansion - instructions

Paraphrased gameplay reference. [Original PDF](rules.pdf) is the authoritative source for this edition; preserve its diagrams and printed exceptions when resolving a dispute. [Inventory and asset links](README.md).

## Contents and requirements

15 cards; base-game player range; illustrated Original-deck setup supports 2-5 players. requires an Exploding Kittens base deck

## Setup

1. Set aside the base deck's four Exploding Kittens and six Defuses, plus this expansion's one Exploding Kitten.
2. Give each player one Defuse. Mix the 14 non-Exploding expansion cards and leftover Defuses into the main deck; for two or three players keep only two extra Defuses and remove the rest.
3. Shuffle and deal seven other cards per player, for eight-card hands.
4. Insert exactly as many Exploding Kittens as players, one more hazard than the normal base setup. Remove unused Kittens.
5. If using Imploding as well, insert it and enough Exploding Kittens so combined hazards equal the player count. Streaking never protects against Imploding.
6. Shuffle, form the face-down draw pile, leave discard space, and choose a first player.

## Turn, survival, and winning

Keep your hand private. On your turn, play any number of playable cards face up onto the discard pile, resolving each one, or pass. Unless a card ends the turn another way, draw the top card to finish the turn. Play starts clockwise. Drawing happens at the end, not the beginning.

Immediately reveal an Exploding Kitten. Use the edition's rescue card as described below or resolve that edition's death procedure. When you Defuse a drawn Exploding Kitten, discard the Defuse and secretly reinsert the Kitten anywhere in the draw pile without looking at or rearranging other cards. That ends one turn; any remaining attacked turns still have to be taken.

The last surviving player wins. Hand size has no upper or lower limit; an empty hand does not eliminate a player. You may count the draw pile. Saving cards early is a suggested strategy, not a rule.

## Combos and timing

A matching pair of cat cards steals one random card. With the PDF's special-combo rules, any two cards sharing a title can do this; three sharing a title let you request a named card from a chosen player, who gives you one if they have it. Ignore the cards' individual effects when spending them as a combo. These PDFs do not establish a universal five-different-card combo.

Nope may cancel a card action or combo before it starts, even outside your turn; another Nope cancels the preceding Nope. Spent and canceled cards stay discarded. Nope does not stop an Exploding Kitten or a rescue card. Cards marked NOW may be used between actions, but not midway through an action. Edition-specific restrictions below take precedence.

Attack transfers the current and any remaining untaken turns and adds the new attack amount. For example, responding immediately to a two-turn Attack with another two-turn Attack gives the next victim four turns; completing one turn first gives them three. Skip ends one required turn; Super Skip ends all of them.

## Edition-specific procedures and variants

- This is an expansion. [Original Edition instructions](../exploding-kittens-original-edition/instructions.md) define normal turns and common card effects.
- Keep Streaking in hand to shelter one Exploding Kitten. Losing Streaking exposes a remaining Kitten; receiving/stealing a held Kitten also causes an explosion. Defuse or die. Defusing a stolen Kitten does not complete the recipient's ordinary turn.
- Putting a held Exploding Kitten into the discard as a played card also explodes you. You cannot spend or recover Streaking/Exploding cards through an older five-card combo.
- Blind Exploding Kitten: if unprotected, play random cards until a Defuse appears; all non-Defuses are lost without effects. If another Exploding Kitten is accidentally played, it too must be defused. After successful rescue, return to non-blind play.
- Blind Favor: if you play Favor, inspect the received card, then place it face down and shuffle it into your blind hand. This exception does not generally allow inspection of your hand.
- Blind Mark: a marked card must face opposite to the rest of the hand even when the hand is blind.
- Blind three-of-a-kind target: find and give the requested card if present, then restore and shuffle your blind hand.
- Blind Garbage Collection: choose a contribution without looking; after selection you may inspect it to detect loss of Streaking protection and resolve any resulting explosion.
- Catomic Bomb affects draw-pile Exploding Kittens only, leaving Imploding where it is. Garbage Collection may accept a sheltered held Exploding Kitten.
- Optional faster variant: after dealing, remove roughly one third of the remaining non-Kitten draw deck without knowing which cards were removed; then add the required number of hazards and shuffle.

## Card reference

The counts in the inventory include cards set aside during setup. Cat names sharing the cat-card family all use the same pair/combo rules.

### Alter the Future 5x

Privately inspect and reorder the top five draw-pile cards, return them face down, and continue your turn.

### Catomic Bomb

Remove and reveal all Exploding Kittens from the draw pile, shuffle the remaining draw pile, and place those Exploding Kittens face down on top. End one turn without drawing. Do not remove Imploding Kittens.

### Curse of the Cat Butt

Choose a player to become blind: place their hand face down and shuffle it. They cannot inspect their cards until successfully drawing without exploding. Cards played while blind are selected randomly and placed face up; resolve playable ones, lose unplayable ones. A blind Exploding Kitten without Streaking protection requires blindly discarding until a Defuse appears; those non-Defuses have no effect. Successfully defusing returns them to non-blind play. An additional accidentally played Exploding Kitten must also be defused.

### Exploding Kitten

Reveal immediately. Defuse or die. Under the Original/Party Pack/Cat Burglar rules, discard your whole hand and the lethal Kitten when eliminated. A Streaking Kitten changes whether one Exploding Kitten may be held; see its exceptions. This is not a normal action you can Nope.

### Garbage Collection

Every player with cards, including you, chooses one card to put secretly into the draw pile; shuffle the pile afterward. A protected held Exploding Kitten may be contributed. Players with empty hands do not contribute.

### Mark

Randomly choose one card in a target player's hand and return it facing outward so others can see it. The owner may still look at it. Keep it marked until it leaves that hand; a stolen card does not stay marked.

### See the Future 5x

Privately inspect the top five cards and return them in the same order without showing others. Continue your turn.

### Streaking Kitten

While kept in your hand, protects one secretly held Exploding Kitten. If Streaking leaves while an unprotected Exploding Kitten remains, Defuse it or die. A player receiving a held Exploding Kitten must Defuse or die; this does not replace their normal turn draw. Playing a held Exploding Kitten into the discard also causes an explosion. Does not protect against Imploding Kittens. In editions with a five-card combo, neither Streaking nor Exploding Kittens can be spent or recovered through that combo.

### Super Skip

End all your required turns without drawing, including outstanding turns from an Attack.

### Swap Top and Bottom

Swap the top and bottom cards of the draw pile without looking at them. This does not end your turn.
